---
title: "Manage invoices and subscriptions"
description: "Find the correct route for billing, cancellation, and refund assistance."
---

Start with your receipt or agreement to identify who processed your purchase. Paddle purchases and direct enterprise agreements can have different support routes.

## Find an invoice or update a subscription

1. Locate the purchase receipt or invoice and check the seller and order reference.
2. For a Paddle purchase, use the subscription or support link in the receipt, or visit [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).
3. For a direct Essal purchase, contact [billing@essal.it.com](mailto:billing@essal.it.com) with the company name and invoice reference.
4. Before confirming cancellation or a change, review the effective date and any remaining access shown in the purchase flow or agreement.

Removing an app account or deleting app data is not a reliable way to cancel a paid subscription. Request cancellation through the purchase channel and keep the confirmation.

## Request a refund

The [Refund Policy](https://legal.essal.it.com/refunds) explains the initial-purchase guarantee, renewal treatment, and mandatory rights. For Paddle purchases, use the receipt support link or paddle.net; Essal can assist through the billing address. Paddle processes refunds for its transactions.

Include the purchase date, company, and order reference. Do not email full card details, passwords, or unnecessary personal data.

For a negotiated enterprise order, also check the signed agreement. This article does not change your contract or promise an exception to the published policy.
