---
title: "Read roadmaps and product updates"
description: "Use statuses and filters without treating a roadmap as a delivery promise."
---

Use a roadmap to understand how the portal owner groups and tracks requests. Use the changelog or updates area to read published product announcements.

## Roadmap

1. Open the roadmap area if enabled.
2. Select the roadmap or board relevant to your product.
3. Use the offered search, sort, and filters to find a request.
4. Open the post and read its current status and discussion before interpreting a column label.

## Product updates

Open the changelog area and read the full entry for a change. Check whether it applies to the product, account, or workflow you use. A mention in a roadmap is different from a published release announcement.

**Expected result:** you can identify the current published status and any available explanation. Roadmap placement and votes do not guarantee a release date, feature entitlement, or contract commitment.

If a page is missing, it may be disabled or restricted by the portal owner. Use [feedback](/roadmaps/feedback-and-votes) for a product suggestion and [support](/roadmaps/support-and-profile) for help with your own account.
