---
title: "Submit feedback and vote on requests"
description: "Find an existing request or write an actionable feedback post."
---

Search before submitting a suggestion so discussion and votes stay together. Feedback posts may be visible to other portal users, so leave private account issues to the support channel.

1. Open the feedback area and search for the feature or problem.
2. Open a matching post and read its description, comments, and status.
3. Use the vote or comment controls offered by the portal. Sign in if requested.
4. If no existing post covers the need, use the submission action and choose an available board or category.
5. Write a specific title, describe the task you are trying to complete, and explain the impact. Add only attachments suitable for the audience.
6. Submit and confirm the post or moderation message shown by the portal.

**Expected result:** your contribution is associated with the intended request or board. Submission may be subject to moderation; it does not guarantee immediate public visibility or a delivery commitment.

If posting or voting is unavailable, check sign-in, portal access, and the controls offered for that board. Do not create duplicate posts to bypass an error. For a private account problem, use [support](/roadmaps/support-and-profile).
