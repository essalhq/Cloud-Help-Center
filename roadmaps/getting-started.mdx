---
title: "Find feedback, roadmaps, and support"
description: "Use the areas enabled in your organization’s customer portal."
---

A Roadmaps portal can combine feedback, a roadmap, product updates, a help center, service status, and support conversations. The portal owner chooses which areas are enabled and who may access them.

1. Open the portal link supplied by your organization or product team.
2. Check the portal name so you do not post to the wrong workspace.
3. Browse the available areas and sign in if the portal requests it for your action.
4. Search existing posts before submitting feedback. Use [feedback and voting](/roadmaps/feedback-and-votes).
5. Use [roadmaps and updates](/roadmaps/roadmaps-and-updates) to follow work, or [support conversations](/roadmaps/support-and-profile) for a private issue where that channel is offered.

**Expected result:** you can find the correct destination for a suggestion, question, or service update. A missing area may be disabled by the portal owner rather than unavailable because of an error.

Do not publish private account details or confidential customer records in feedback posts. Visibility and access can differ between a public feedback board and a support conversation.
