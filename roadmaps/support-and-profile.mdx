---
title: "Use portal support and account preferences"
description: "Keep private issues in support and review your portal profile."
---

Use the portal’s support area, when enabled, for questions that should not appear on a feedback board. Confirm the portal belongs to the organization you want to contact.

1. Sign in if requested and open the support area.
2. Look for an existing conversation about the same issue before opening another.
3. Describe the product, action, error, and time. Include only information needed to investigate.
4. Submit through the available conversation controls and confirm the message appears in the intended thread.
5. Return to that conversation to read replies rather than posting private details as public comments.

## Profile and preferences

Use the account settings available in the portal to review your profile and notification preferences. Check the saved result after a change. Your portal profile is separate from the membership or employee record managed in another app.

If the support area is not offered or you cannot access it, use [Essal’s support contact](/introduction/contact). Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or provider credentials. For urgent security concerns, follow your organization’s incident process as well.
