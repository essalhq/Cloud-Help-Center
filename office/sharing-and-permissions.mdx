---
title: "Share Office documents and control editing"
description: "Review people, link access, and viewer or editor permissions."
---

Sharing controls determine who can read or edit a document. Review both named access and link sharing before sending a URL.

1. Open the resource and its sharing dialog.
2. Review the people who already have access and the role assigned to each.
3. Add or update access only when you are authorized to do so. Choose viewing when editing is unnecessary.
4. Review whether link sharing is enabled and the access role attached to the link.
5. Copy the intended link and ask the recipient to confirm they can open the resource with the expected permissions.

**Important:** anonymous visitors remain read-only; editing requires sign-in and permission. A selected link role is not proof that an unsigned visitor can edit.

If a recipient cannot open the document, confirm the account, resource, space, and current sharing settings. If you disable link sharing, people who have separate named access may still retain it. Review both routes when removing access.

Do not enable broad sharing to troubleshoot a single colleague’s access problem. Grant the specific access needed and follow [safe sharing](/privacy/secure-sharing).
