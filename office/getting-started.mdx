---
title: "Start using Essal Office"
description: "Find your space, create a document, and choose how to share it."
---

Essal Office provides a workspace for documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and other available resource types. Start by choosing the personal or team space where the work belongs.

1. Sign in with the account invited to your workspace.
2. Select the intended space and review its folders and resources.
3. Open an existing document or use the create action for a document type offered in that space.
4. Name the document and confirm it appears in the intended location.
5. Follow [documents and folders](/office/documents-and-folders) to organize work, then review [sharing and permissions](/office/sharing-and-permissions) before inviting collaborators.

**Expected result:** you can reopen your document from the correct space. A read-only resource may open without showing editing controls.

Import and export availability depends on the document type and enabled capabilities. Use the formats actually offered in the menu; see [import and export](/office/import-and-export).

If a resource disappears after deletion, check [Trash and recovery](/office/trash-and-recovery) before recreating it. Do not assume a personal space and a team space give the same access to colleagues.
