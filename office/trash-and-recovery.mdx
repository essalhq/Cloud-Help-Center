---
title: "Restore a deleted Office resource"
description: "Review Trash and distinguish restoration from permanent deletion."
---

Check Trash when a resource was deleted from a space. If it is merely missing from a list, first confirm the space, folder, filters, and permissions.

1. Open the relevant space’s Trash view.
2. Find the deleted item or group and check its name and context.
3. Use the restore action available to your role.
4. Return to the resource list and confirm the restored content is accessible.
5. Review sharing after recovery before sending its link again.

**Expected result:** restoration returns the recoverable item to the workspace. If it is not listed or restoration fails, stop and [contact support](/introduction/contact) with the space and resource details.

## Permanent deletion

Permanently removing an item from Trash is different from sending it to Trash. Read the confirmation and check whether the selection contains multiple resources. Do not purge content if you are still investigating a missing document or need a colleague to recover it.

This article does not promise a universal recovery period or restoration after a permanent deletion. Availability depends on the item’s state and the operations offered by your workspace.
