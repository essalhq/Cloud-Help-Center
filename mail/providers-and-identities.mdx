---
title: "Connect Mail providers and identities"
description: "Configure an authorized provider and verify the intended sending identity."
---

A provider connects an email service to a workspace. An identity represents a domain or email address used with that service. Configuration requires appropriate administrative access and authorization over the service and domain.

1. Open the workspace’s provider settings and choose a supported provider shown by the app.
2. Follow that provider’s form and supply only credentials intended for this workspace. Do not copy credentials into a support message.
3. Review the connection or verification result before creating dependent identities.
4. Open the identities area and add the domain or email identity supported by your chosen provider.
5. If DNS verification is required, give the exact records displayed by the app to the domain administrator. Verify them through the app after they are available.
6. Assign identity access to the people who need it and perform a non-sensitive send/receive check.

**Expected result:** the provider and identity show the required verified or connected state, and authorized users can open the mailbox.

Do not replace existing DNS records blindly. Domain authentication and routing changes can affect other mail services. If verification fails, compare the record name, type, and value with the app before retrying.

For a colleague who sees no mailbox, check [getting started](/mail/getting-started) and identity access before changing provider credentials.
