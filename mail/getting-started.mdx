---
title: "Start using Essal Mail"
description: "Choose a workspace and mailbox before sending or organizing messages."
---

Essal Mail organizes mail around a workspace, connected providers, and email identities. A workspace login does not automatically give access to every mailbox.

1. Sign in using your organization’s Mail link and choose the intended workspace.
2. Open the email identity or mailbox assigned to you. Check the sender address before composing.
3. Read a message and confirm the mailbox and folder you are viewing.
4. Follow [sending and organizing messages](/mail/messages-and-attachments) for your first conversation.
5. If no mailbox is available, ask the workspace administrator to check the identity and your access.

**Expected result:** you can open an authorized mailbox and identify the address used for outgoing mail.

Administrators can use [providers and identities](/mail/providers-and-identities) to connect an email service. Keep provider credentials private; ordinary mailbox users should not need to configure them.

Use [rules and signatures](/mail/rules-and-signatures) for repeatable mailbox behavior, and [Mail troubleshooting](/mail/troubleshooting) if sending, receiving, or synchronization fails.
