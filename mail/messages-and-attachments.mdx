---
title: "Send and organize Mail messages"
description: "Review sender, recipients, and attachments before sending a message."
---

Choose the correct mailbox before composing or replying. Shared access to a mailbox makes checking the sender and recipients particularly important.

1. Open the intended mailbox and choose the compose action, or open a conversation to reply.
2. Check the sender identity and recipients. Review copied recipients before using reply-all.
3. Write a clear subject and message. Add attachments through the composer and wait for the upload result.
4. Review the recipient addresses, content, and attachments before sending. Use only file types and sizes accepted by the composer.
5. Check the sent view or any displayed error before resending. A network interruption is not proof the first attempt failed.

## Organize your inbox

Use the folder, search, label, archive, and other actions available in your mailbox. Confirm which messages are selected before using a bulk action. Archiving and deletion have different effects; follow the confirmation shown by the app.

**Expected result:** the message appears in the appropriate mailbox view and the composer reports no unresolved error. Delivery to another person’s inbox can still depend on the receiving service.

For missing messages or failed attachments, use [Mail troubleshooting](/mail/troubleshooting). Never forward authentication codes or confidential records just to test whether sending works.
