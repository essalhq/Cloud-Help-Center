---
title: "Check service status"
description: "Distinguish a service incident from a local access or device problem."
---

Check the [Essal Status Page](https://status.essal.cloud) when several people cannot use a service or the same action repeatedly fails.

1. Find the component or service affected and read any incident updates.
2. Compare the incident time with the time your problem started.
3. Follow any published workaround and keep unsaved work before refreshing or signing out.
4. If no relevant incident is listed, check your connection, organization, and permissions using [common troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/common-problems).

A status page is not a guarantee that your account, workspace, or device has no separate issue. Contact [support](/introduction/contact) with the app and error details if the problem continues.

For Access devices working offline, reconnect and confirm synchronization before clearing storage or reinstalling the app.
