---
title: "Contact Essal support"
description: "Send a useful support request without exposing credentials or private records."
---

Email [support@essal.it.com](mailto:support@essal.it.com) for product faults, account access, or technical assistance. If your app offers a support form, you can use it to describe the same issue.

## Include these details

- The product and organization or workspace involved.
- What you tried to do and the steps that caused the problem.
- The expected result and what happened instead.
- The error text and the approximate time, including your timezone.
- The browser or device involved and whether other colleagues are affected.

If you include a screenshot, hide personal records, card details, sign-in links, passwords, and tokens. Do not send a full employee or candidate export unless support has established a suitable secure channel and a need for it.

## Use the right contact

- Invoices, cancellation, or refunds: [billing@essal.it.com](mailto:billing@essal.it.com).
- Privacy requests, legal complaints, or abuse reports: [legal@essal.it.com](mailto:legal@essal.it.com).
- Problems with workplace permissions: contact your organization’s administrator first.

Check [service status](/introduction/status) for known incidents. For an urgent security concern, also follow your organization’s incident process; an ordinary product-support request is not an emergency response channel.

The [Complaints Policy](https://legal.essal.it.com/complaints) explains escalation. Any contracted support commitments are governed by your agreement.
