title: "Use Guard security-awareness guidance"
description: "Find the right training contact and report security concerns through an established channel."
---

Guard’s customer interface is organized around security awareness, including training and reporting concerns. It is different from Access badge scanning and should not be described as a central identity provider or a guarantee of automated threat response.

## Before using Guard

Ask your organization’s administrator which Guard workflows are enabled for your account and how completion or submitted reports are recorded. Example courses, campaign charts, and risk displays do not establish that your organization has assigned training, launched a campaign, or received a report.

1. Use the Guard link supplied by your organization and confirm your account.
2. Open the training area offered to your role and follow the material your organization has assigned.
3. Confirm with the training owner how to record completion and where to ask questions.
4. If a displayed action does not save or an assignment is missing, report the exact screen to your administrator or [Essal support](/introduction/contact).

## Report a security concern

For a suspicious email, lost device, or other incident, use your organization’s established security reporting channel. Do not rely on a dashboard message or example report as proof an incident responder has been notified. Do not open suspicious links merely to collect evidence.

Provide the time, affected service, and a short description through an approved channel. Keep passwords, authentication codes, and unnecessary personal information out of the report.
