Use the Access link provided by your organization. The administrator dashboard, employee portal, and check-in device are separate experiences; choose the one intended for your role.

## Signing In

1. Open the organization’s Access sign-in page.
2. Enter the email address associated with your invitation or account.
3. Choose the email-link or password method offered on the page. Use the most recent sign-in message and never forward it to another person.
4. Complete the verification and confirm the organization shown after sign-in.

If password recovery is offered, use **Forgot password** and follow the new message. If a link is expired or already used, request another from the same app. Ask an administrator to verify a suspended account or missing invitation.

## The Admin Dashboard

The dashboard summarizes badge and scan activity for the selected organization. Review the available metrics and recent events, then open the appropriate area for the task:

- **Employees:** find, add, or import employee records according to your role.
- **Guest Badges:** manage temporary visitor credentials.
- **Passes:** issue and review temporary or purpose-specific access. Older links may call these Tickets.
- **Scan Logs:** investigate scans and their results.
- **Audit Logs:** review recorded operational and administrative events.
- **Settings:** review organization configuration available to your role.

Badge design is an Admin-only area. See [roles and permissions](/access/getting-started/understanding-user-roles) rather than assuming every sidebar item is available to every account.

## If you reach the wrong place

An Employee account should use the organization’s Employee Portal link. A paired check-in device has its own setup and operator controls. Do not enter an administrator password into a public verification page.

For an unresolved login issue, [contact support](/introduction/contact) with the app, organization, time, and error. Exclude passwords and sign-in links.


## Need help?

If an action is missing, check [your role](/access/getting-started/understanding-user-roles) and the active organization. Use [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/common-problems) or [contact support](/introduction/contact) with the exact message.
