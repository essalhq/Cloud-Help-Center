Essal Access separates organization administration from employee self-service. Assign a role according to the person’s actual responsibilities, then verify the pages and actions they can use.

## The Four Roles at a Glance

| Role | Dashboard access | Important limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin | Organization dashboard and administration | Badge Designer requires this role. Review the organization before changing shared settings. |
| Manager | Dashboard, employees, printing, guests, scan logs, passes, users, settings, public-page and business-card editors, audit logs | Page visibility does not grant every administrative action. Badge Designer is not available. |
| Security | Dashboard, employee list, guest badges, scan logs, audit logs | Operational access; employee management and organization configuration are restricted. |
| Employee | Employee Portal | Does not receive administrator-dashboard access just by having an employee record. |

## Admin

Use Admin for people responsible for the organization’s configuration and privileged actions. The Badge Designer is restricted to Admin in the current app. Review the scope of a template change before saving because it affects the shared badge design.

## Manager

Managers support day-to-day work. They can open several operational and settings pages, but a visible page does not guarantee permission to invite, suspend, delete, or change every record. Use the actions offered for the current account; ask an Admin when a required control is unavailable.

## Security

Security staff use the dashboard, employee lookup, guest badges, scan logs, and audit logs for verification and investigation. Share only the employee or visitor information needed for the task.

## Employee

Employees use the portal link supplied by their organization to view their badge and the profile or business-card actions enabled for them. Ask an administrator to check the link between the login and employee record if the portal shows no badge.

## Assigning and reviewing access

1. Confirm the correct organization and person.
2. Open the available system-user controls with an authorized account.
3. Choose the least privileged role needed and confirm the change.
4. Have the person sign in with their own account and verify the expected access.

If a page is missing, check the role before reporting an outage. Do not share an Admin login or assume a role in another Essal product applies here.


## Need help?

If an action is missing, check [your role](/access/getting-started/understanding-user-roles) and the active organization. Use [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/common-problems) or [contact support](/introduction/contact) with the exact message.
