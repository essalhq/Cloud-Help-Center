The organization using an Essal app may control workplace or applicant records. Essal also handles information for its own accounts, support, and business purposes. The [Privacy Policy](https://legal.essal.it.com/privacy) explains these roles and your rights.

## Choose the right route

- For an employee record, job application, or organization-owned document, contact the employer or organization responsible for it.
- Use an in-app deletion or privacy request option when your app offers it. Confirm the organization and the scope before submitting.
- For Essal’s own processing or help routing a request, contact [legal@essal.it.com](mailto:legal@essal.it.com).

Describe the app, organization, record type, and action requested. Use only the information necessary to identify the request. We may need to verify identity before responding.

## Before deleting records

Deletion, suspension, and revoking a shared link have different effects. Check the app’s confirmation, ownership, and recovery options before proceeding. Account deletion does not automatically cancel billing or remove records that another controller must retain.

Do not assume deleting a visible record instantly removes every copy or legally retained billing record. Ask about the scope of the request if this matters to you.

## Optional analytics

Where a consent control is offered, choose whether to allow optional analytics. See the [Cookie Policy](https://legal.essal.it.com/cookies) for the distinction between essential functionality and optional analytics. Browser settings can affect stored preferences.
