---
title: "Share records and links safely"
description: "Review recipients and public visibility before sharing workplace information."
---

A public badge, visitor credential, document link, and invitation do not provide the same access. Review the destination and audience before sharing any link.

1. Open the resource and check what a recipient will see.
2. Choose named recipients or the narrowest sharing setting offered for the task.
3. Give editing access only when needed. Confirm whether a link can be opened without signing in.
4. Share through an approved channel and ask the intended recipient to confirm access.
5. Revoke access when the task, visit, or employment relationship ends.

For Access badges and visitor links, confirm identity, validity, and the public fields displayed. For Office documents, check the viewer/editor distinction in [sharing settings](/office/sharing-and-permissions). For Talent, protect candidate documents and interview information.

Treat passwords, invitation links, authentication codes, provider credentials, and webhook addresses as sensitive. If one is exposed, ask your administrator to revoke or replace it and report the exposure through your organization’s security process.
