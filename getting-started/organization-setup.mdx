---
title: "Set up your organization"
description: "Prepare your workspace, invite the right people, and complete a first task."
---

Set up one app at a time using an account authorized to administer your organization. Settings and roles are product-specific; changing a profile in one app does not prove it changed everywhere.

1. Sign in and confirm the organization or workspace name.
2. Review the organization settings available to your role. Set the name, branding, language, and time settings where offered.
3. Invite a colleague with the minimum role needed for their work. Have them accept the invitation using their own address.
4. Complete a small first task using information you are authorized to enter:
   - Access: [create an employee record](/access/employee-management/adding-a-new-employee).
   - Talent: [prepare a job](/talent/jobs-and-career-page).
   - Project: [create a project and task](/project/projects-and-tasks).
5. Confirm your colleague can see only the resources they should access.

**Before inviting a wider team:** review sharing settings, administrative roles, and any public pages. Use [members and permissions](/account/members-and-permissions) to resolve missing access.

If you cannot find an organization you expect to join, ask its administrator to verify your membership rather than creating a duplicate workspace.
