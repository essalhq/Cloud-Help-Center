title: "Sign in or recover your account"
description: "Choose the correct app, accept an invitation, and resolve sign-in problems."
---

Use the application link supplied by your organization. A marketing website, an employee portal, and an administrator sign-in page can lead to different experiences.

## Sign in

1. Open your organization’s app link and confirm the product name.
2. Use the email address that received your invitation. Choose the password, email-link, or organization sign-in method offered on that page.
3. Complete any verification requested by the app. Open a sign-in email on a device and browser you control.
4. Check the organization or workspace shown after signing in before changing records.

**Expected result:** you reach the workspace or portal associated with your account. An employee account does not necessarily grant administrator access.

## If the email or link does not work

- Check spam and confirm the address matches your invitation.
- Request a fresh link from the same app. Use the most recent email instead of repeatedly opening an older one.
- If password sign-in is available, use **Forgot password** to start recovery. Organization-managed sign-in may require your IT administrator.
- Ask an administrator to confirm that your invitation is still valid and your account is active.

If you still cannot enter, [contact support](/introduction/contact) with the app name, time, and error message. Never send a password, sign-in link, recovery code, or session token.
