title: "Manage invitations and permissions"
description: "Understand product-specific roles and safely resolve missing access."
---

An invitation grants access to a particular app or workspace. It does not automatically grant the same role across Essal products.

## Invite or update a member

1. Confirm the organization you are administering.
2. Open its member or user settings. Only use actions available to your administrative role.
3. Check the recipient’s address and choose the least privileged role that supports their work.
4. Send the invitation and ask the recipient to accept it with that address.
5. Review the member’s status and verify their access to the intended resources.

## Roles differ by product

- **Access:** Admin, Manager, Security, and Employee have different dashboard and portal access. The badge designer requires Admin. See [Access roles](/access/getting-started/understanding-user-roles).
- **Project:** administrators manage the workspace; members can create and collaborate; viewers have restricted editing. Task editing can also depend on assignment. See [Project permissions](/project/members-and-settings).
- **Talent:** organization membership and hiring workflows are managed separately from candidate records. See [Talent settings](/talent/team-and-settings).
- **Office:** sharing a document with a viewer does not grant editing rights. See [Office sharing](/office/sharing-and-permissions).

If an action is missing, confirm the app, workspace, role, and resource permissions. Ask an administrator to grant the specific access needed rather than sharing an administrator’s credentials.

Before removing or suspending a colleague, check ownership of shared work and coordinate any necessary handover.
