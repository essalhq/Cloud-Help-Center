Essal Talent helps a hiring team manage jobs, candidates, applications, and interviews. Older help links may use Careers or Hire. Start in the organization that owns the recruitment process.

## First steps for a hiring team

1. Sign in with your invited address and confirm the active organization.
2. Open **Jobs** to review existing roles before creating another one.
3. Prepare a role using [jobs and the career page](/talent/jobs-and-career-page).
4. Open **Candidates** or **Applications** to find the person and the role involved. A candidate profile and an application are different records.
5. Use [candidate and application guidance](/talent/candidates-and-applications) to review information and progress the hiring workflow.
6. Check [team settings](/talent/team-and-settings) before inviting additional recruiters.

**Expected result:** you can identify the correct job, its applicants, and the next action assigned to your team.

## Applicants

Apply through the employer’s public job page and follow the fields and privacy notice shown there. Questions about a hiring decision or correcting an application belong to the employer. Essal product support cannot make a recruitment decision for that organization.

If a page or action is unavailable, verify your organization and membership. Do not create duplicate candidate records to work around a permissions problem.
