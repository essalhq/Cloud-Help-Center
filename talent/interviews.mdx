---
title: "Schedule and review interviews"
description: "Confirm participants, timing, and responses before treating an interview as scheduled."
---

Use the interview workflow to coordinate the candidate and hiring team. Confirm the candidate and application before creating an appointment.

1. Open **Interviews** and the scheduling action available to your role.
2. Select the candidate or application and review the participants.
3. Enter the date, time, timezone, and location or meeting details requested by the form.
4. Review the invitation content before sending or confirming. Check the resulting interview status and participant responses.
5. Before the meeting, verify the latest schedule and link. Record relevant feedback after the interview through the controls provided.

**Expected result:** the interview record shows the intended participants and timing. An interview created in the app is not proof that every participant has accepted or received an invitation.

If a time changes, use the available update or cancellation workflow and confirm the candidate received the revised details. Do not create a second appointment without checking whether the first remains active.

If a response or invitation is missing, check the recipient address, displayed status, and integration errors. Contact your organization administrator for calendar or email settings; do not share provider credentials in a support request.
