---
title: "Review candidates and applications"
description: "Find the right record, review evidence, and manage a hiring stage."
---

A candidate is a person’s profile. An application connects that person to a job and its hiring process. Check both before changing a stage or recording a decision.

1. Open **Candidates** to find the person or **Applications** to find a submission for a particular role.
2. Confirm the name, contact details, organization, and job. Search before manually adding or importing a candidate to avoid duplicates.
3. Review the documents and information the applicant supplied. Keep interview notes relevant to the role.
4. Use the application or pipeline controls to record the next stage offered by your app.
5. Check the resulting state and coordinate the next action with the hiring team.

## Importing candidates

Use the candidate import screen when available. Read its format requirements and validation results before confirming. Review matching and duplicate warnings rather than treating a successful file upload as proof every row was imported correctly.

## Assessment and decisions

If your organization enables analysis or matching assistance, use it as one input and review it against the candidate’s evidence. Recruiters remain responsible for the decision and the message sent to the applicant. Do not assume an analysis result automatically rejects, contacts, or advances someone.

For correction or deletion requests, follow your organization’s recruitment privacy process and [data-request guidance](/privacy/manage-data).
