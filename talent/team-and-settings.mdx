---
title: "Manage your hiring team and settings"
description: "Review memberships, public notices, and organization settings in Talent."
---

Organization settings determine who can work in your hiring space and how your public recruitment pages appear. Use the correct organization and an authorized administrative account.

1. Open **Settings** and review **Members** before inviting another person.
2. Check existing members and pending invitations. Choose the role needed for the colleague’s work and verify the address.
3. Send the invitation through the app and confirm its status. Use available member actions to review or change access when responsibilities change.
4. Review the career page and localization settings before publishing organization-wide changes.
5. Review the privacy and retention settings with the person responsible for applicant data. Configure the notice and contact appropriate to your organization.

## Sensitive settings

SSO, email or calendar integrations, analysis settings, and retention controls can affect the whole organization. Read the displayed requirements before saving. Do not treat a demonstration value or an unsaved form as an active policy.

Retention settings determine how your organization handles applicant records; do not assume a Help Center article overrides them or your legal duties. See [privacy requests](/privacy/manage-data) for the correct contact route.

If a setting is unavailable, verify your role and any entitlement message shown by the app. Contact [support](/introduction/contact) with the screen and error, excluding secrets.
