---
title: "Track and review your work time"
description: "Use the enabled time module and review entries before reporting."
---

Use Project’s time area when your workspace enables it. Confirm the workspace and the task or project before recording effort.

1. Open **Time** from the workspace navigation.
2. Use the timer or entry action available on your screen. Associate the record with the appropriate work and add a useful description.
3. If you start a timer, stop it when you stop working on that activity. Check for an active timer before starting another.
4. Review the recorded date, duration, and associated project or task. Correct an entry using the controls allowed by your role.
5. Open the reports view when available and select the intended period before interpreting totals.

**Expected result:** the entry appears in the correct period and work context. A timer still running is not the same as a reviewed record.

If an entry seems missing, check the date range, workspace, filters, and any save error before recording the same time again. If you cannot change an entry or open reports, ask the workspace administrator about your permissions.

Time totals support your organization’s workflow. Do not assume they independently establish payroll, invoicing, or approval without your organization’s process.
